Installer's guide

Wiring the coin slot to a PC

Four wires and a power plug. The coin slot talks to the PC through the serial (COM) port's control pins, so no extra board is needed.

Step by step about 15 minutes

  1. Power. Coin slot DC12V to the Molex's yellow (+12 V) and GND to its black.
  2. Coin signal. Split the COIN wire in a Y to DTR (pin 4) and DSR (pin 6). Female dupont jumpers push straight onto the pins.
  3. Accept / stop. RTS (pin 7) to the coin slot's SET, the upper (+) pin. RapTech uses it to stop the slot taking coins it cannot credit.
  4. COUNTER is not needed.
  5. On the coin slot set the switch to NO and the speed to SLOW, and program each coin to send one pulse per peso.
  6. In RapTech: Staff panel → Coin slot, pick the COM port, press Connect, drop a real coin. The count should go up one per peso.

No count? Flip "The machine's switch is set to NO". One coin counted as several? Raise the shortest signal. A shaky built-in port? A USB-to-RS232 adapter works and shows up as another COM port.

Board header COM / JCOM

No port on the back? Use the motherboard's 10-pin COM header:

2RXD
4DTR
6DSR
8CTS
10none
1DCD
3TXD
5GND
7RTS
9RI

Same three: 4 DTR, 6 DSR, 7 RTS. Find pin 1 (the square pad) in your board's manual first; layouts can differ.

How it works: RapTech holds DTR on to power the loop, the coin slot closes it once per pulse, and DSR counts each pulse. RTS switches the slot on and off.
