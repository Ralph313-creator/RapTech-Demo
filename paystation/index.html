What the customer sees at the station

A working mock of the 7-inch touch screen on the Orange Pi (1024 × 600). Tap through it as a customer would. Use the panel on the right to drop coins, feed bills, cut the network or switch a PC off, and watch how the screen answers. Example rate card: ₱5 = 30 m, ₱10 = 1 h, ₱20 = 2 h, ₱50 = 5 h 30 m.

How the station is wired

The coin slot and the bill acceptor are read as pulses through an optocoupler board, so their 12 V never touches the board's 3.3 V pins. A two-channel relay switches each one off, which is how the slots stay off until the counter answers.

Planned wiring: the board is still to be chosen, and each acceptor's datasheet decides pulse or serial and which inhibit level enables it.
